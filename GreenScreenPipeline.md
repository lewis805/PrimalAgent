LEWIS BLAKELOCK — GREEN SCREEN CONTENT PIPELINE

## Purpose

Find trending mainstream media stories from the last 7-10 days — on any topic — and turn them into green screen reel scripts in Lewis's voice. The story does not need to be about fitness. It needs to be something people are already talking about. Lewis then bridges from that story to the health consequence for his ideal client.

**The formula:**
> [Thing everyone is watching/reading about] → here's what that actually means for your health → here's what to do about it

The story grabs attention. The bridge earns relevance. The script converts.

---

## STEP 1 — SEARCH FOR TRENDING STORIES

Use web search to find stories published in the last 7-10 days. Cast a wide net. Any category is valid as long as a bridge to fitness/health exists.

**Sources to check:**

UK news: BBC News, Daily Mail, The Sun, Sky News, The Guardian
US news: CNN, New York Times, Fox News, TMZ, New York Post
Tech/AI/business: TechCrunch, The Verge, BBC Tech, CNBC
Celebrity/entertainment: People, Ladbible, Daily Mail sidebar, E! News
Fitness/health press: Men's Health, Women's Health, Business Insider Health, Healthline

**Categories — any of these qualify:**

- **General world news** — geopolitical conflict, economic shifts, energy prices, cost of living, policy changes. Anything that affects the daily behaviour of working professionals.
- **AI and technology** — AI replacing jobs, screen time, remote work, productivity tools, wearables, digital burnout
- **Economy and cost of living** — inflation, petrol prices, housing, commuting costs, spending habits
- **Celebrity body/lifestyle** — weight loss, training routine, transformation, someone famous talking about their health or appearance
- **Scientific or health studies** — new research on diet, sleep, longevity, exercise, obesity, metabolism, stress
- **Cultural/political moments** — obesity crisis debate, NHS policy, food labelling, sugar tax, Ozempic, workplace wellness, alcohol culture
- **Viral trends** — anything spreading on social media that touches behaviour, lifestyle, body image, performance, or time

---

## STEP 2 — FIND THE BRIDGE

For every story, ask: **what is the direct health or fitness consequence for a 40-year-old corporate professional who already has too much on his plate?**

The bridge does not have to be obvious. That is the point. Lewis sees the connection others miss.

Examples of how the bridge works:

- Petrol prices spike → people drive less → unintentional step count increases → easiest fitness opportunity of the year and most people aren't using it deliberately
- AI anxiety news → professionals reporting higher stress about job security → cortisol is elevated for months → this is exactly when body composition gets worse, not better
- New conflict overseas → oil supply disruption, energy prices rising → cost of living pressure → financial stress is the number one thing killing sleep in your demographic right now
- A celebrity looks visibly unhealthy despite being "in shape" → the thing people celebrate as discipline is what's destroying them
- New study on sitting time → office workers average 10 hours seated → nobody mentions what that does to testosterone over 10 years

The bridge must be specific to Lewis's ICP: men, 35-55, corporate or business, time-poor, high stress, professional success while health slowly declines.

---

## STEP 3 — FILTER CRITERIA

Only keep a story if:

- A busy professional aged 35-55 would have seen or heard about it
- A credible bridge to health/fitness exists — even if indirect
- Has a visible headline, graphic, or stat that works as a green screen background
- The angle has not been done to death already

Discard anything:

- So niche only specialists would recognise it
- Where the bridge to health is a stretch
- More than 10 days old

## STEP 5 — GENERATE THE GREEN SCREEN REEL SCRIPT

Once Lewis selects a story, produce the script.

Before writing, re-read Voice+Script.md in full.

**Script format:**

At the top, include:

```
[GREEN SCREEN: paste or describe the visual — headline text, article screenshot, stat graphic]
```

Then the script itself, structured as:

**Hook** — sharp, direct, makes the viewer stop. Pull it from the news headline or from the fitness angle, whichever is more arresting. Do not write the hook — extract it.

**Body / Explain** — set up the news story in one or two sentences. Plain English. Assume the viewer knows nothing. Then pivot: here's the thing everyone missed.

**Bridge** — the moment the reel earns its place. Connect the news story to the specific health reality of Lewis's audience. This must feel like an insight, not a forced segue. Specific, grounded, real.

**Illustrate** — make it land emotionally. Put the viewer in a real situation. What does this actually look like for a 44-year-old sales director who hasn't trained in eight weeks? Use a real scenario, a contradiction, a social cost, a physical consequence.

**Teach / Takeaway** — one clear, actionable thing. Not a motivational statement. Something the viewer can actually do or think differently about.

**CTA** — only include if it fits naturally. Not forced.

**Word count:** 120-180 words. Strict.

**Tone rules — follow Voice+Script.md exactly:**
- Sounds spoken, not written
- No banned phrases, no guru language, no abstract filler
- Grounded in lived experience and specificity
- British register throughout
- Authority from observation and reality, not performance
