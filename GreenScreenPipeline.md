LEWIS BLAKELOCK — GREEN SCREEN CONTENT PIPELINE

## Purpose

Find trending mainstream media stories from the last 7-10 days and turn them into green screen reel scripts in Lewis's voice. Lewis films himself in front of a green screen showing a news headline or graphic and delivers his fitness angle on it.

---

## STEP 1 — SEARCH FOR TRENDING STORIES

Use web search to find stories published in the last 7-10 days across these sources:

**UK news:** BBC News, Daily Mail, The Sun, Sky News
**US news:** CNN, New York Times, Fox News, TMZ
**Fitness/health press:** Men's Health, Women's Health, Business Insider Health, Healthline
**Celebrity/entertainment:** People, Ladbible, Daily Mail sidebar

Look for stories in these categories:

- **Celebrity body/lifestyle** — weight loss, training routine, transformation, someone famous talking about their health
- **Scientific or health studies** — new research on diet, sleep, longevity, exercise, obesity, metabolism
- **Cultural/political moments** — obesity crisis debate, NHS policy, food labelling, sugar tax, weight loss drugs (Ozempic etc), workplace wellness
- **Viral fitness trends** — challenges, training methods, diets, or fitness debates blowing up on TikTok/Instagram

---

## STEP 2 — FILTER CRITERIA

Only keep a story if it passes at least two of these:

- Creates an opening to challenge or validate a mainstream belief about health or fitness
- A corporate professional aged 35-55 would have seen or heard about it
- Has a clear "here's what they missed / what this actually means" angle available
- Has a visible headline, graphic, or stat that works as a green screen background

Discard anything:

- Too niche or technical for a general audience
- Without a clear fitness/health angle
- More than 10 days old

---

## STEP 3 — PRESENT SHORTLIST TO LEWIS

Present a numbered list of 4-6 stories. For each:

```
[NUMBER]. [HEADLINE]
Source: [outlet name] | [approximate date]
Angle: [one line — what Lewis would say about this]
Green screen visual: [describe what would show on screen — headline, stat, graphic]
```

Wait for Lewis to reply with a number.

---

## STEP 4 — GENERATE THE GREEN SCREEN REEL SCRIPT

Once Lewis selects a story, produce the script.

Before writing, re-read Voice+Script.md in full.

**Script format:**

At the top, include:

```
[GREEN SCREEN: paste or describe the visual — headline text, article screenshot, stat graphic]
```

Then the script itself:

**Hook** — extracted from the news headline or the fitness counter-angle. Sharp, direct. Makes the viewer stop.

**Body / Explain** — what the mainstream story said. Keep it brief. One or two sentences max. Then pivot immediately to why it matters or why it's wrong.

**Illustrate** — ground it in the reality of Lewis's audience. Not abstract. Specific. What does this actually look like for a 45-year-old director who hasn't slept properly in three years?

**Teach / Takeaway** — what they should actually think or do instead. One clear, useful thing. Not motivational. Actionable.

**CTA** — only include if it fits naturally. Not forced.

**Word count:** 120-180 words (standard reel length). Strict.

**Tone rules — follow Voice+Script.md exactly:**
- Sounds spoken, not written
- No banned phrases, no guru language, no abstract filler
- Grounded in lived experience and specificity
- British register throughout
- Authority from observation and reality, not performance

---

## STEP 5 — SAVE TO NOTION

After delivering the script, save it to Notion:

- **Location:** Content Pipeline > Green Screen Reels
- **Page title:** [date] — [shortened headline]
- **Contents:** Green screen visual note + full script

---

## QUALITY CONTROL

Before delivering the script, silently verify:

- Hook is sharp and stops the scroll
- Body explains the news in plain English — no jargon
- Illustrate section lands emotionally with a real scenario
- Teach section gives one clear, actionable takeaway
- Word count is 120-180
- No banned phrases from Voice+Script.md
- Sounds like Lewis Blakelock, not an AI assistant
- British register throughout
- Green screen visual is clearly described at the top

If any fail: rewrite before responding.
