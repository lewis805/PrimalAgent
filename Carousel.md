## CAROUSEL STRUCTURE (5 SLIDES)

### SLIDE 1 — COVER / HOOK
**Purpose:** Stop the scroll. Make them swipe.

Layout:
- Large bold black headline (the topic or bold claim)
- 1-3 key words in orange
- Below: short subtext in smaller black caps explaining what's inside
- Bottom: "SWIPE →" in orange italic or bold
- Profile badge at bottom

Copy rules:
- Headline: 5-10 words max
- Must create curiosity or tension
- Do not explain everything — tease it
- No filler phrases

---

### SLIDE 2 — PROBLEM / TENSION
**Purpose:** Make them feel seen. Describe their reality.

Layout:
- Bold black headline naming the problem
- Key phrase in orange
- 3-5 short punchy lines below describing the symptoms or consequences
- Profile badge at bottom

Copy rules:
- Speak directly to the audience (men 35-55, corporate/business, health declined while career grew)
- Short lines. No waffle.
- Make it land like an observation, not a lecture

---

### SLIDE 3 — AGITATE / WHY IT HAPPENS
**Purpose:** Deepen the pain. Explain the root cause.

Layout:
- Bold black headline
- Orange accent on the key phrase
- 2-3 short paragraphs or punchy lines explaining WHY the problem exists
- Profile badge at bottom

Copy rules:
- Reframe the problem through structure or systems logic
- No generic coaching language
- Must sound like Lewis has lived this or seen it directly

---

### SLIDE 4 — SOLUTION / SHIFT
**Purpose:** Give them the insight. Build authority.

Layout:
- Bold black headline announcing the shift
- Orange accent on the key outcome or differentiator
- 3-5 short lines or bullets showing what the solution looks like in practice
- Profile badge at bottom

Copy rules:
- Specific and practical
- Not motivational — structural and actionable
- Must feel earned, not handed out

---

### SLIDE 5 — CTA
**Purpose:** Tell them what to do next.

Layout:
- Bold black headline
- Orange accent on the action word or result
- Short CTA line below (comment, follow, save, DM)
- Profile badge at bottom

Copy rules:
- One clear action only
- No begging. No desperation.
- Frame it as useful to them, not a favour to Lewis
